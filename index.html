Why this matters now

A rejected document costs a listing, a shipment or a retest. Most of the facts that decide it sit in Chinese-language systems.

  • CPSC withdrew accreditation from four China-based labs on 15 January 2026 after finding unreliable or falsified results. Reports from those labs no longer support a US certificate. CPSC statement
  • Marketplaces have tightened which labs they accept. Sellers report rejections for labs outside the accepted list or without ISO 17025 accreditation, and for EU and UK toys the lab now submits the report directly.
  • Many rejections are mismatches, not fakes. The report names a supplier's internal model, a different variant, or a superseded standard version. Those are checkable before you submit.

What we check

Three checks per report. Each result is backed by a screenshot or a written confirmation from the lab.

1

The lab

Is the lab real and accredited for this test? We check CNAS, ILAC member bodies, the CPSC accepted-lab list and the EU NANDO database, including the accreditation scope.

2

The report number

Does the report exist in the lab's own system? We use the lab's Chinese-language verification portal, or call and email the lab in Chinese with your written authorisation.

3

The match to your product

Do the model, variant, sample photos, applicant, brand name and standard version match what you sell and what you are submitting?

Questions

Why can't I just ask the lab myself?

You can. Many Chinese labs run their verification portals and phone lines in Chinese only, and often do not answer emails from buyers they don't know. That is the gap we fill.

What if the lab won't confirm?

We mark the item "Not verifiable" and say why. We never label a report fake without evidence.

Will my supplier know?

Only if you want them to. We contact the lab, not your supplier, unless you ask us to.

What happens to my documents?

They are used only for your check, never shared or used to train any model, and deleted 30 days after delivery.
